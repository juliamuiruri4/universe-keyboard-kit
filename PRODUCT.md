# Universe Keyboard

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js. Static output and hash routing for GitHub Pages subdirectories.

## Users

People who got the GitHub Universe keyboard kit, or are curious about it, and want to see how it goes together.

## Product Purpose

A realistic, interactive keyboard build. Start from the sealed blind box (the real photo, unmodified, as the scene), open it, see the parts piled inside, take them out onto the wooden floor around it, assemble the board, test the keys (with a live typing preview). A short guide embeds the walkthrough video once. A gallery shows the 34 photos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The keyboard is the first-viewport subject on desktop and mobile; the next action is always one tap away.
- Ordered steps: open box and take out parts, stabilizers, switches (dampener and plate first), housing, screws, keycaps, test.
- Every action can be undone; build is saved locally; reduced motion is honored.
- Exactly one click-to-play YouTube embed, in the guide. No other video links or "watch this step" calls.
- Colors are limited to what the video states: Light or Dark keycaps. No invented colorways.
- Photos are never modified; no push or deploy before manual review.
- Never access SharePoint or the original photo folder.

## Brand Commitments

GitHub brand: neutrals with GitHub Green (#0FBF3E) in small, purposeful moments. Mona Sans. Precise, confident, plainspoken. Purple and blue brand colors are not used for UI. Hardware colors (pink housing, gray caps, blue accents) are product material, not brand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Walkthrough: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q3Jod8Xu8BY. The transcript names Light or Dark keycaps, blue accent keycaps, blue plate, clear top housing, red bottom housing; short Backspace without stabilizer; three stabilizers; eight donut bumpers, six isolation strips, four screws. It does not list the full range of kit colors.
- Manufacturer page: omnitype.com/products/bauer-lite. 331.7 × 124.8 mm, 5° typing angle, polycarbonate housing, hot-swap PCB.
- Photos in `public/images/`, including `R0002873` packaging (Omnitype Bauer Lite blind box) and the photographed board.

## Product Principles

- Show the real object. Keep copy short.
- One focused instruction at a time.
- State what is verified; say nothing about what is not.
